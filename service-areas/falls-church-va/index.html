Building in Falls Church

City or county?

The City of Falls Church is an independent city of about two square miles. Many Falls Church addresses outside it are in Fairfax County, which issues its own permits. We confirm which one applies before we price the job.

Mature trees

Big trees are part of why people live here, and their roots are the most common reason a walk or driveway has heaved. We route around major roots where we can, and tell you where a tree and a hard surface cannot share the same ground.

Replacing what is there

Most work here is replacing an original walk, driveway, or set of steps rather than building on open ground. Tear-out, base repair, and what happens to the old material are all in the estimate.

Frequently asked

A tree root has lifted my walk. Can you fix it without killing the tree?

Often. We can reroute the walk, bridge a root, or use a surface that tolerates some movement. Where a large root has to be cut, we tell you first, and suggest an arborist look at it.

Is my Falls Church address in the city?

Only part of the Falls Church mailing area is the City of Falls Church; much of it is Fairfax County. Your real-estate tax bill shows which. We confirm it at the site visit.

Do you haul away the old driveway?

Yes. Tear-out and disposal are itemized in the estimate, so you can see what they cost.
